What We Do

Comprehensive Waste Management Solutions

From routine clinical waste collection to emergency hazardous spill response — we provide the full spectrum of medical and industrial waste management services, tailored to your facility's needs.

Clinical & Infectious Waste Collection

Our core service — scheduled collection of sharps, blood-contaminated materials, pathological waste, and microbiological specimens from hospitals, clinics, and laboratories. We supply colour-coded UN-certified containers and provide regular, reliable collection cycles customised to your facility's volume and risk profile.

Request a quote

High-Temperature Incineration

Pathological, pharmaceutical, and highly infectious waste is destroyed at our licensed incineration facility operating above 850°C — eliminating pathogens and reducing waste volume by over 95%.

Learn more

Autoclave Sterilisation

Steam sterilisation under high pressure renders reusable instruments and certain waste streams safe. Our autoclaves are calibrated, logged, and validated to international sterilisation standards.

Learn more

Chemical & Hazardous Waste

Laboratory chemicals, reagents, solvents, and industrial effluents require specialist handling. We provide compliant transport, neutralisation, and disposal for all categories of chemical waste.

Learn more

Pharmaceutical Waste Disposal

Expired medications, cytotoxic drugs, and controlled substances cannot simply be discarded. We manage pharmaceutical waste in full compliance with Ministry of Health and NEMA guidelines.

Learn more

Emergency Spill Response

Hazardous spills demand immediate, expert response. Our rapid-response teams are equipped with full PPE and specialist absorbents to contain, decontaminate, and safely dispose of hazardous material spills.
